Our Humble Beginnings

From a small startup to a comprehensive technology solutions provider

The Foundation

AMATETA Techs & Designs was founded with a vision to bridge the gap between technology and business needs in Namibia. We started by offering essential IT support services to local businesses.

Rapid Growth

As client needs evolved, we expanded our expertise to encompass digital marketing, web development, and creative design services. Our team grew to include specialists across multiple domains.

Today & Beyond

Today, we're a trusted partner for businesses across Namibia, delivering comprehensive technology solutions that drive growth, efficiency, and digital transformation.

Our Mission

To empower businesses with innovative technology solutions that drive growth, enhance efficiency, and create meaningful digital experiences. We strive to be the trusted partner that helps our clients navigate the digital landscape with confidence.

Our Vision

To be Namibia's leading technology partner, recognized for excellence in delivering comprehensive digital solutions that transform businesses and contribute to the growth of the local economy through innovation and expertise.

Our Core Values
